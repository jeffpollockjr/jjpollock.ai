Every engagement is fixed-scope and plainly priced before work starts. Not sure which one fits? That's what the free intro call is for.

AI Opportunity Audit

2 weeks · the starting point

Most AI audits hand you a slide deck of vague ideas. Mine ends with a roadmap ranked by ROI and one small working prototype, so you can see the value before committing to a build.

  • Interviews with you and the people doing the work
  • Workflow mapping and a tools-and-data inventory
  • Every AI opportunity scored by ROI and feasibility
  • A written roadmap plus one small thing that actually runs
  • A readout meeting where we pick what, if anything, to build

100% of the audit fee credits toward a build signed within 90 days.

Custom No-Code & Low-Code Workflows

1–4 weeks · fixed scope

The fastest wins usually live between the tools you already pay for. I build automations with platforms like Zapier, Make, and n8n that move data, draft documents, and kill copy-paste work - no engineering team required.

  • One workflow, built end to end, in your accounts so you own it
  • Lead intake, invoicing, scheduling, reporting, follow-ups, and more
  • A written playbook so your team can maintain it
  • One success metric agreed in writing before work starts

AI Training & Workshops

Half day · your whole team

The easiest offer to say yes to. One half day gets your whole company speaking the same language about AI: what it can and can't do, how to catch hallucinated output, and how to avoid leaking company data through prompts.

  • Live demos on your team's own workflows, not generic examples
  • Hallucinated-output pitfalls and how to check AI's work
  • Data-leak safety: what never goes into a prompt
  • Q&A and a take-home prompt guide for your team

Custom AI Solutions

2–6 weeks · fixed scope

When no-code isn't enough, I build the custom piece: an assistant that answers questions from your own documents, an internal tool your team actually uses, or an integration between your systems and an AI model.

  • Assistants trained on your documents, policies, and history
  • Internal tools and dashboards built around one workflow
  • Integrations between your existing software and AI models
  • Documentation and training so it runs without me

AI Governance & Policy

1–2 weeks · written deliverables

Your team is already using AI, whether you've written the rules or not. I write the governance chapters for your company handbook: clear, practical rules people will actually follow.

  • An acceptable-use policy: which tools, for what work
  • Data-handling rules: what never leaves the building
  • A review process for AI-generated output before it reaches customers
  • A vendor-vetting checklist for new AI tools
