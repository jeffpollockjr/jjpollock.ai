How it plays

Easy to learn. Hard to stay safe.

1

Draw

Pick up cards from the deck at the start of your turn.

2

Play up to two

Play goat cards, or launch an action at your rivals.

3

Keep your hand to seven

Then pass the turn. Final orbit starts when the last card is drawn: everyone gets one more turn.
